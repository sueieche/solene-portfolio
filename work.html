02.01 / Knives

骨子 Knives

攝影 × 文字 · 2025 個展 The first solo exhibition of Solène Su Cheng — image and text as a single breathing organism, presented at butter room, Macao.

02.02 / Tears

Tears Collection

抒情攝影 · 2022 · 影像詩 Tears as form, as texture, as body. A series of lyrical photographs made in private — the work that prefigured the 2025 KNIVES exhibition.

02.03 / Folded Pages

摺頁文字牆

裝置 · butter room · 2025 Folded pages hung in sequence. A wall of texts becomes an installation, becomes a room you can read with your body.

02.04 / anti.detox

@anti.detox

私寫 · Instagram · 2018— Private writing since 2018. Notes on intimacy, image, the body, the edge. The archive of a sensibility before it became public work.

02.05 / Tulle

紗簾裝置

互動裝置 · 2020 · Archive An early interactive piece using tulle as a permeable membrane — the body of the viewer becomes part of the work.
