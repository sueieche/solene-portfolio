01 關於創作者 About the Artist Biography 自述

Solène Su Cheng（程昭漫），澳門理工大學多媒體設計系畢業。2018 年起以帳號 @anti.detox 持續發表私寫文字，並以「素」為筆名撰寫劇評刊於網上平台《評地》。2022 年曾於北京從事時尚造型工作，現回澳投身影像與文字的創作。她的實踐橫跨私密攝影（KNIVES）與抒情攝影（Tears Collection），始終把「文字」與「影像」當作兩種不能分離的媒介。

Solène Su Cheng (程昭漫) is a Macau-based artist and writer who graduated from the Macao Polytechnic University in Multimedia Design. Since 2018, she has been publishing private texts under the handle @anti.detox, and writing theatre criticism under the pen name "素" for the online platform 評地. After a year of fashion styling work in Beijing (2022), she returned to Macau to focus on photography and writing. Her practice moves between private portraiture (KNIVES) and lyrical photography (Tears Collection). She treats image and text as two inseparable media.

「大概也是生命中這樣不能坦露的時刻，埋在骨裡成為引子，造成人與人之間的吸引，導致你抱我，我抱你的時候，像抱著碎掉的自己一樣。」 "Perhaps it is also the unlivable moments of a life that get buried in the bone, become the kindling for attraction — so that when you hold me, I hold you, it is like holding a broken self."

04 推薦引言 Selected Press Press / Essays 推薦引言
