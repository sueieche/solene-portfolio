01 Artist

Solène Su Cheng（程昭漫），澳門理工大學多媒體設計系畢業。2018 年起以帳號 @anti.detox 持續發表私寫文字，並以「素」為筆名撰寫劇評刊於網上平台《評地》。2022 年曾於北京從事時尚造型工作，現回澳投身影像與文字的創作，把「文字」與「影像」當作兩種不能分離的媒介。

Solène Su Cheng (程昭漫) is a Macau-based artist and writer who graduated from the Macao Polytechnic University in Multimedia Design. Since 2018, she has been publishing private texts under the handle @anti.detox, and writing theatre criticism under the pen name "素" for the online platform 評地. After a year of fashion styling work in Beijing (2022), she returned to Macau to focus on photography and writing. She treats image and text as two inseparable media.
